# Reply to the CORPES coordinator (draft)

Context: Josh's courtesy email of August 30, 2026 (see `prompts/frequency-ranks-for-all-verbs.md`,
Step 7) described the CORPES XXI credit as if it were already in the App Store build. The
coordinator downloaded the app, looked for the credit, and could not find it, because the credit
and the CORPES-based ranks live on the `frequency` branch and have not shipped yet. This reply
apologizes for the confusion, says both will appear in an upcoming version, and points to the
attached screenshots of the Credits screen in Spanish and English.

Attach two screenshots before sending: the Credits screen (Info tab ▸ Credits) scrolled to the
"Frecuencias de los verbos" section with the simulator in Spanish, and the same section in
English ("Verb frequencies").
