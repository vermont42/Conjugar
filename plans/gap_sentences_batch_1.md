# Complete the Sentence: sentences to review (118)

Each blank has passed the machine check, which confirms it against the engine in tú
and in vos. What it cannot judge is the rest: whether the Spanish is natural in both
Spain and Latin America, whether the sentence makes its person plain, and whether the
English says the same thing. A tú sentence is asked in vos to a learner whose setting is
vos, so it names no tú, ti, or contigo.
