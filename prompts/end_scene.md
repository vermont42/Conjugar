The end scene of Toreo por Amor is fantastic, but it can be even better. Here is the plan.

Every five seconds, the scene changes. First, an image of the matador, dancer, and bull is shown. Second, an image of the developer, Josh; his wife, Amanda; and their horse, Vegas is shown. Third, the current end scene is shown. The transition from one to the next is fancy. The next scene or image grows out in an expanding circle at the center of the screen. Josh has seen this transition in movies but does not know what it is called.

In the dancer/matador/bull scene, all three are facing the viewer. The bull is between the two humans and has a white garland around his neck. The dancer, matador, and bull are smiling.

In the Josh/Amanda/Vegas scene, all three are facing the viewer. Vegas is between the two humans and has a white garland around his neck. Josh, Amanda, and Vegas are smiling.

This project requires crafting prompts for gemini-image and gpt-image. Here are some details or those prompts. Reference images for the bull, dancer, and matador already exist as app icons. Reference images for Vegas, Josh, and Amanda are in ~/Downloads/horse.jpg and ~/Downloads/couple.jpg . The looks of the bull, dancer, and matador should match those of the app icons. Vegas's look should match his reference image but with a white garland. Josh's and Amanda's looks should match their reference images but with clothing colors changed to match those of the dancer and matador. With respect to aspect ratio, use 3:2.

The image should be draggable in game so that, for example, on iPhone, the user can see the whole image in portrait mode. A toast saying "Drag Me" should briefly appear on screen.
