After one second, the note explodes with a yellow-and-blue particle effect.

* Cortejo: When the player jumps, a small mushroom flies to the enemy affected by the power-up. This is similar to how hearts fly with the flechazo power-up.

* Flechazo: When the player fires a heart, a heart appears, over .3 second, to the right of the matador, at his vertical center. When the player's heart missile hits an enemy, the matador's heart explodes with a yellow-and-blue particle effect.
